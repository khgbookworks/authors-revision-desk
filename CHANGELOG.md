# Change log

## Author-controlled editing workspace

Added a separate editing mode with selectable editing goals, conservative browser-side suggestions, visible reasons, editable proposed wording, per-change Approve and Reject controls, bulk decisions, local persistence, mobile-friendly layout, and comparison exports for TXT, HTML, Word-compatible review, and JSON backup. Original manuscript text remains preserved until the author approves a suggestion.

## Beta reading and formatting expansion

This change separates reader perspective from book formatting. Beta reading is suggestion only and does not rewrite manuscript text. Book Formatter handles finished manuscripts and presentation choices without changing their words.
