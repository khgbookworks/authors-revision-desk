Apply the notes

Concrete revision moves

These suggestions translate the reading into changes you can make on the page.

“

The chapter gives good advice. With deeper examples and a more personal sense of risk, it could give the reader an experience they remember.

01

Write the scene first

Draft a 250 word opening scene before the current introduction. Do not explain the lesson inside the scene. Let the details carry the pressure.

02

Give the author a place in the room

Add one honest paragraph about what you have witnessed, learned, misunderstood, or regretted. It will create trust more quickly than general authority.

03

Build a difficult case

Show a company with a product customers like but a culture that pressures recruitment. Ask what professionalism requires there.

04

Make the standards precise

Add a short checklist covering customer demand, refunds, required purchases, income disclosures, training costs, and recruitment pressure.

05

Trade repetition for evidence

Keep the strongest line about trust, service, and integrity. Use the space saved for dialogue, a mistake, or a concrete customer interaction.

06

Protect vulnerable readers

Remind readers not to take on debt, buy unnecessary inventory, or rely on future income to cover present expenses.
