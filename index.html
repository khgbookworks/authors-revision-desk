A simple professional process

Read deeply. Decide deliberately.

Good feedback is not a verdict. It is material you can examine, question, and use. Formatting comes later, when the words and voice are settled.

01

Capture the manuscript

Import a text file or paste your chapters. The original draft stays untouched.

02

Listen as a reader

Record what you felt, expected, questioned, and would suggest trying next.

03

Choose as the author

Decide what belongs in the work. The app does not make that decision for you.

04

Format when ready

Move the finished manuscript to the formatter and export a reading file without rewriting it.

A note on privacy

Your draft is not a product.

This app is designed to work without an account or a manuscript server. Your project lives in this browser until you export it. Clear your browser data and the local project may be lost, so use Backup project regularly.
